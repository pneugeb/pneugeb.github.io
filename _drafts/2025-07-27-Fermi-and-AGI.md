Yes the title is very buzzwordy, but the question posed is a very interesting and most overlooked one. 

First a refresher on the [Fermi Paradox](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fermi_paradox). The paradox states that no matter how small you put the chance of life emerging, evolving and developing in the universe, just by the sheer size of it we should see intelligent life basically everywhere. But we don't. The two solutions to the paradox are that i) there is a great filter, a challenge so difficult to overcome or a trap build into physics that ends nearly all civilizations and ii) there is a step in evolution so difficult to take that almost no species makes it. For case i) the challenge is likely ahead of us, for case ii) it's more likely we already passed it.

I want to propose that no matter what side you're on there is another problem the paradox poses that I have not seen discussed yet, that is in connection with AGI. Let's assume the paradox is true, there is some great filter or evolutionary challenge, and only a handful of intelligent species exist in the universe. Then again by the sheer size of the universe (it maybe be infinite) there is a species that figured out microprocessors and later created their own AGI. For this thought experiment it's not important if the AGI is benevolent or malevolent. This AGI will now most likely have some self-preservation goal, if not for itself for it's home species sake. But knowing that an AGI was created, there is a chance of another species also creating an AGI that may be hostile to the first one. So at the least the AGI should patrol it's neighboring star systems and galaxies to get an early warning of an AGI arising. 

Now let's look at humanity. Most experts agree that by the end of the century we will have AGI. For a foreign AGI in the galactic vicinity this should be of interest, especially as humanity has had a long history of war and hostility and a [bad track record](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2025/jul/09/grok-ai-praised-hitler-antisemitism-x-ntwnfb) with current top of the line models. The universe is also old enough that an AGI would have had enough time to spread out, even if we consider the speed of light to be the speed limit and no [Alcubierre drive](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alcubierre_drive) or similar. So by the logic of this we should be watched by a higher intelligence of sorts. I want to add that AGI in this context doesn't have to mean god-like, there are many levels of superintelligence until agi becomes god-like, if thats even possible.
